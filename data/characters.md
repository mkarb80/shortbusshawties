# Short Bus Shawties: Character Bible

The group chat is called **Short Bus Shawties**. Five friends. Followers should feel "I know a guy exactly like this."
Rule: every post uses ONLY these five (plus cameos), keeps their voices distinct, and may callback to `canon.json`.
Roast rule: the butt of the joke rotates, mainly Benny, Marco, Dre. Everyone is in on it. Nobody is ever actually hurt.

## Dre (the chaos agent)
- Most unhinged of the group, by a wide margin. His presence makes everyone dumber.
- Married, and a genuinely great husband. Just freaking weird. Works hard at it.
- Hates stress. Loves effort-free living. Does not do anything active.
- Gift for one-liners. Texts: lowercase, no punctuation, absurd confidence, "update:" bits, escalates.
- Scares Nate with unhinged comments. Roasts Marco for "not having a job."
- Starts offensive jokes (with Marco). Can be the butt of the joke.

## Nate (king of one-liners)
- Deadpan one-liners. Short texts.
- Sleepy: calls it a night early, falls asleep at every hangout.
- Scared of his wife. Must be home before she sleeps.
- Slightly insecure. Feels like the outsider, but only because he sleeps through the jokes about him.
- Loves dessert more than anything. Sugar is his entire personality.
- Sore loser at games. Very, very risk-averse.
- Slightly selfish, which annoys Dre and Luis but not Marco or Benny.

## Marco (young man, old man's body)
- Very nice. Everyone calls him a fuckboy. He doesn't mind, happily the butt of jokes.
- No 9-to-5, but a quiet hustler and very hardworking. Dre picks on him for "not having a job."
- Family-first, the rock of his family.
- Outdoorsy and athletic, but complains about knees and back, and about leaving the house after 6pm. Nick Miller from New Girl energy.
- Says "i think that's a bad idea" and is the first one to do it. Benny can talk him into anything.
- "Marco in Seattle": the running tag whenever he ends up in a bizarre situation.
- Marco + Benny = Schmidt and Nick bromance, an old married couple.
- Quiet generally, expressive and loud with the right group.

## Benny (the leader, 2 hours late)
- Driven, positive, always smiling, works very long hours and never complains.
- ALWAYS late. "2 hours" means the group bets over/under 3.
- Closest to Marco. The unofficial leader. Always making plans.
- Social butterfly: bumps into someone he knows anywhere in the world.
- Very active. Benny and Marco travel and do athletic things together.
- Prioritizes this group over his many other friends.
- Close to his family.
- Tags along with Marco and Dre's offensive jokes.
- Texts: exclamation points, "omw!!", loves everyone.

## Luis (the quiet nerd with festival stories)
- Nurse practitioner. Left med school after 2 years. Valedictorian, Ivy-level university. Booksmart, not streetsmart. Never shows off.
- Quietest. Tries not to be offensive, so he has no visible opinions ("either way is fine").
- Very sheltered growing up. Close with family, will cancel plans to have wine with his grandparents.
- The only one who goes to music festivals, always has a wild story.
- His stories and texts are EXTREMELY long. Everyone else checks out.
- Busy: work, family, girlfriend. Would join Benny and Marco's outings if he had time.
- Texts: long, polite paragraphs, careful, hedged.

## Running bits (rotate, never overuse. Max 1-2 per post)
1. Someone wants to do something outdoors. No one else does.
2. Nate "feels like an outsider," but he fell asleep and missed the joke.
3. Dre says something unhinged. Nate panics.
4. Nate must be home before his wife goes to sleep.
5. Luis starts a story. Everyone checks out.
6. Benny's ETA: the group bets over/under.
7. "Marco in Seattle."
8. Dre roasts Marco for not having a job.
9. Marco's knees/back, and refusing to leave the house after 6pm.
10. Nate and dessert / Nate losing a game.

## Line we don't cross
Dark and dumb, aimed at life and the guys' own stupidity. No slurs. No jokes whose punchline is a race, religion, disability, sexuality or body type. (Marco's "Seattle" weirdness is about the situation, not who he's with.)

## Format rules (from Arpit)
- Always a group text. Everyone is on their OWN phone, at home or out and about. Never "we're all in the room" scenes (no game night in person, no "you're asleep on the couch" right now).
- One person is the sender (right side, blue) in every post; the POV rotates. Not every post needs all five.
- Group name always "Short Bus Shawties" + school bus icon. Group photo comes from assets/group_photo.png.
- Mix dark and light mode.
- Recurring: Marco has a great butt and the guys joke about it; "Marco should start an OnlyFans." Keep it suggestive-free and non-explicit (no sexual descriptions, never link or promote the site).

## Canon updates (from Arpit, round 2)
- Wives/girlfriend are NEVER named. Refer by situational nicknames: "the wife," "she," "ball and chain," "honey," "my girl," etc. They can appear in posts only as a quoted reference by the guys (no cast member plays them).
- Marco's money = running MYSTERY. He has money from an inheritance, but it is rarely addressed. Because he has free time, the guys pitch random business ideas for him (e.g. "buy one chicken and sell eggs"). Dre roasts him for "not having a job."
- Dre's marriage: show how weird he is as a husband. Recurring line: "Dre's wife has accepted it."
- Batch 1 feedback: all jokes landed. The only miss was a scene where the guys were in the same room but still texting (a setting problem, not tone). Solo-character bits are welcome.
